What is a digital credit card?

A digital credit card is a card number stored securely on your device, ready for online payments, mobile wallets and subscriptions — without plastic.

  • Virtual card numbers (VCN)
  • Instant issuance after approval
  • Works with Apple Pay / Google Pay
  • Better control for online purchases
  • Replace numbers without replacing the card

Digital vs. physical card

Digital

Instant use · Virtual numbers · Wallet-ready · Higher security online.

Physical

Works everywhere · Tap-to-pay · ATMs · Legacy compatibility.

Security benefits

Digital cards use advanced tokenization technology:

  • Network tokens replace the real card number
  • Dynamic CVV for online protection
  • Virtual numbers per merchant
  • Biometric authentication
  • Full control from the app

Mobile wallet compatibility

Digital cards integrate seamlessly with:

  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • Samsung Wallet
  • Garmin / Fitbit Pay
